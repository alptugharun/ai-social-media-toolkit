# Influencer Fit Auditor

Evaluate influence by **fit and business value**, not follower count alone.

## Evidence

Use public profile data, media kits, campaign data, comments, content, and user-provided analytics when available.

Do not infer precise audience demographics from appearance or vibes.

## Fit Dimensions

Score qualitatively or numerically only when supported:

- Audience alignment
- Topic relevance
- Engagement quality
- Content quality
- Brand safety
- Sponsorship saturation
- Production quality
- Conversion evidence
- Usage-rights value
- Reliability / professionalism
- Platform fit

## Creator Review

Capture:

- Creator
- Platform
- Audience size
- Typical format
- Recent content topics
- Relevant outliers
- Comment quality
- Brand partnerships
- Audience / geography evidence
- Potential risks

## Deal Review

Check:

- deliverables
- posting window
- approval / revision rules
- disclosure
- exclusivity
- content usage rights
- whitelisting / creator ads
- tracking method
- payment structure
- kill / rework terms

## Measurement

Prefer:

- qualified traffic
- tracked conversions
- affiliate / promo code performance
- content reuse performance
- retained customers
- post-campaign brand search / survey signals when available

Do not treat EMV or raw reach as the only outcome.
