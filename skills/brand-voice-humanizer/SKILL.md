# Brand Voice Humanizer

Improve voice without changing truth.

This is not a synonym spinner.

The goal is to remove generic AI texture while preserving meaning, factual claims, and the creator's recognizable style.

## Read Context First

If creator context exists, use the brand voice rules before rewriting.

## Common AI Texture to Remove

Watch for:

- empty scene-setting
- repetitive summaries
- symmetrical list spam
- generic motivational language
- inflated adjectives
- unnecessary disclaimers
- robotic transitions
- identical paragraph lengths
- fake certainty
- overuse of "in today's world"
- over-explaining obvious points
- CTA language that sounds templated

## Preserve

Do not alter without evidence:

- names
- dates
- numbers
- quotes
- legal meaning
- product claims
- technical facts
- source attribution

## Rewrite Process

1. Identify the actual point.
2. Identify the intended reader.
3. Preserve factual payload.
4. Rebuild sentence rhythm.
5. Replace generic abstractions with concrete language when supported.
6. Remove repetition.
7. Restore personality without becoming sloppy.
8. Read aloud mentally for spoken flow when the output is a script.

## Output Modes

### Light
Keep structure; fix obvious AI texture.

### Editorial
Rewrite for stronger flow and human authorship.

### Spoken
Optimize for natural voice-over or camera delivery.

### Brand-Locked
Follow creator-context voice rules strictly.

## Final Check

Ask:

- Does this sound like a person with a point of view?
- Did any factual meaning change?
- Is the rhythm natural?
- Are there unnecessary phrases?
- Does the platform fit remain intact?
