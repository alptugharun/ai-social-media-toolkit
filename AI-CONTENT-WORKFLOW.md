# AI Content Workflow

A practical framework for researching, planning, producing, reviewing and distributing AI-assisted social media content.

Created by **Alptuğ Harun**.

This workflow is designed for creators, social media professionals and digital teams who want to use artificial intelligence without removing human strategy, creative judgment or editorial control.

---

## Workflow Overview

The system follows nine stages:

**Research → Strategy → Hook → Script → Visual Direction → Production → Human Review → Distribution → Measurement**

AI can accelerate several stages of the process.

However, the final decisions should remain human-led.

---

## 1. Research

Content production should begin with research rather than generation.

Research may include:

- Audience questions
- Search behavior
- Platform trends
- Competitor content
- Community discussions
- Seasonal demand
- Industry developments
- Emerging visual formats

The objective is not to ask AI:

> What should I post today?

The objective is to give the system enough context to understand **why a topic deserves to become content**.

---

## 2. Strategy

Before creating the content, define its role.

Every piece of content should have a purpose.

Examples:

- Awareness
- Education
- Discovery
- Engagement
- Authority building
- Website traffic
- Lead generation
- Product discovery
- Brand positioning

A useful structure:

**Audience → Platform → Format → Objective → Call to Action**

Without this layer, AI often produces generic content.

---

## 3. Hook Development

The first seconds of a video or the first lines of a post determine whether the audience continues.

Useful hook structures include:

- Curiosity
- Problem
- Contrarian idea
- Unexpected fact
- Transformation
- Question
- Mistake
- Comparison
- Demonstration

A practical workflow:

**10 AI-generated hooks → 3 shortlisted hooks → 1 human-selected hook**

---

## 4. Script

Once the hook is selected, develop the script around one clear idea.

A simple structure:

**Hook → Context → Value → Example → Conclusion → CTA**

AI can help with:

- Drafting
- Shortening
- Rewriting
- Structural alternatives
- Tone adaptation
- Platform adaptation

The creator should still control the final voice.

---

## 5. Visual Direction

Visual production should not begin with random image generation.

First define:

- Subject
- Composition
- Camera angle
- Lighting
- Environment
- Color direction
- Style
- Platform format

For campaigns or visual series, consistency matters.

**Keep the scene logic consistent while changing only the creative variable that needs to change.**

---

## 6. Production

An example production stack:

**Research**  
ChatGPT • Gemini • Grok • NotebookLM

**Writing**  
ChatGPT • Claude

**Design**  
Canva • Adobe Photoshop • Adobe Illustrator

**Video**  
CapCut • DaVinci Resolve

**Voice**  
ElevenLabs

**Automation**  
Zapier • AI Agents • Custom Workflows

The objective is not to use the maximum number of tools.

The objective is to use the **smallest effective tool stack**.

---

## 7. Human Review

AI-assisted content should pass through a human review stage before publishing.

Review:

- Facts
- Dates
- Statistics
- Sources
- Brand voice
- Repetition
- Visual consistency
- Platform suitability
- Copyright risks
- Hallucinations
- Call to action

Human review is especially important when content includes current events, health, finance, legal information or public claims.

---

## 8. Distribution

One strong idea can become multiple platform-native assets.

Example:

**Research Topic**

↓

**Instagram Reel**

↓

**Instagram Carousel**

↓

**Pinterest Pins**

↓

**LinkedIn Post**

↓

**Blog Article**

↓

**Short Video Variations**

This should not be simple copy-paste repurposing.

Each platform should receive a version adapted to its audience and discovery system.

---

## 9. Measurement

Useful metrics may include:

- Reach
- Impressions
- Watch time
- Completion rate
- Saves
- Shares
- Comments
- Profile visits
- Link clicks
- Search impressions
- Conversions

The next content cycle should use these signals.

**Create → Publish → Measure → Learn → Improve**

---

## Core Principle

Do not use artificial intelligence simply to produce more content.

Use it to build a better content system.

A sustainable AI-assisted workflow should improve:

**Speed + Consistency + Research + Creativity + Measurement**

without sacrificing:

**Accuracy + Originality + Human Judgment**
