## Distribution rules

- one useful post per channel, not repeated spam;
- lead with a concrete result, not "please star";
- only ask for a star after showing value;
- do not mass-DM strangers;
- do not post identical copy everywhere;
- do not claim users, installs, stars or traction that are not visible;
- respond to real questions with examples, not sales copy.
