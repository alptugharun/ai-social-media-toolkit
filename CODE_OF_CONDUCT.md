# Code of Conduct

This community exists to help people learn, build and improve practical AI workflows together.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas, code and workflows rather than people.
- Share reproducible context when reporting problems.
- Clearly distinguish experiments, assumptions and verified behavior.
- Respect privacy, licensing and platform rules.
- Welcome beginners and non-developers who are trying to complete real work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Hate speech or discriminatory abuse.
- Spam or repeated unrelated promotion.
- Star-for-star, fake engagement or manufactured adoption.
- Publishing credentials, private client data or confidential information.
- Deliberately misleading claims about results, compatibility or production readiness.

## Scope

These expectations apply to repository Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions and other project spaces controlled by the maintainers.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content that does not follow these rules and may limit participation when necessary to keep the community useful and safe.

If a post contains a security vulnerability or sensitive credential, do not continue the discussion publicly. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
